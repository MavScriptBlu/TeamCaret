# Team Caret Ground Rules

## Team Values

- Treat all team members with respect and professionalism.
- Communicate openly and honestly.
- Be willing to give and receive constructive feedback.
- Support each other and ask for help when needed.
- Everyone is responsible for contributing to the project.

## Communication and Meetings

- Team members should respond to important team messages in a reasonable amount of time.
- We will communicate any issues or delays as soon as possible.
- Team members should attend scheduled meetings or let the team know if they cannot attend.
- Standups are due by 11:00 PM Tuesday through Thursday.
- Standups should briefly include what was completed, what is being worked on, and any blockers.

## Decision-Making and Accountability

- Give everyone an opportunity to share their ideas before making decisions.
- Major decisions should be discussed with the team rather than made by one person.
- Complete assigned work by the agreed-upon deadline.
- If someone is falling behind, the team will work together to find a solution rather than assigning blame.
